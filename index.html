// 01 — ABOUT

About

I'm Shah Faisal — a student and developer who learns best by shipping. Most of what I know about programming came from breaking things in public repos, then fixing them well enough to be useful to someone else.

Outside of coursework, I founded and run Eleone Hub, an open source community built around one idea: the best way to get better is to build alongside other people, in the open, with your work reviewable by anyone. I spend most of my time between that community, personal projects, and figuring out what to learn next.
